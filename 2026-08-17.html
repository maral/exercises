How to read a dose & run a block

“3 × 10 / side” means 3 sets, and each set is 10 left + 10 right. That’s 30 per side in total, not 10. Do left, then right, inside the same set — don’t do all three sets on the left and then switch.

“5 × 10 s” = 5 holds of 10 seconds. “2 × 45 s / side” = 2 holds of 45 seconds on each side.

Then the block tells you how to sequence them

Tap the coloured badge on any block to see this again. Three kinds:

STRAIGHT SETS — finish one exercise completely, then start the next.

Goblet squat 3 × 8
  8 reps → rest 2–3 min
  8 reps → rest 2–3 min
  8 reps → done, move to DB RDL

ALTERNATE — the two exercises take turns. The other exercise is your rest, which is the only reason this fits in 30 minutes.

Pair A = leg lowering + glute bridge
  round 1: leg lowering (10 L, 10 R) → 15 s → bridge (12 L first, 12 R) → 15 s
  round 2: same
  round 3: same
  then: bridge 4th set, LEFT ONLY
  → 3 sets of each, done

FLOW — warm-ups and the sink work. Do the listed reps once through each exercise, no rest, keep moving.

Running out of time? Cut from the bottom of the day, never the top. The groin and left-hip work is the last thing to drop.
